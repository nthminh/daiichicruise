1 · Chính sách hoàn huỷ

Cancellation policy · キャンセル規定 · 취소 정책 · 取消政策 · Annulation

EN: Free cancellation up to 12h before departure (transport & day cruises). Overnight cruise: 100% >7 days, 50% >3 days, non-refundable within 72h except weather bans. Refunds in 5–7 working days.
